# Code Of Conduct

TraceGate is intended to be a practical, technical open-source project. Contributors are expected to keep discussions respectful, specific, and focused on improving the project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct without being hostile.
- Critique ideas, code, docs, and tradeoffs rather than people.
- Assume good intent, but ask for clarification when something is unclear.
- Keep security and safety discussions concrete and responsible.
- Respect maintainers' scope decisions for the current phase.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Repeated off-topic or disruptive comments after being asked to stop.
- Encouraging unsafe deployment patterns without clear warnings.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block contributors when behavior harms the project or community. Reports should include relevant links, screenshots, or context so maintainers can review them fairly.
